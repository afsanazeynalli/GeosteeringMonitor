Use your own data Paste or import CSV

Paste CSV with a header row: md,tvdss,top,base and optionally gr,phi,rhob,rt,rop. Values in metres (TVDSS negative downward). Target sliders are ignored for custom data.

CSV format reference

One row per MD sample along the lateral, first line is a header. Columns are matched by name (any order, any case). Separator: comma, semicolon or tab. Depths in metres, TVDSS negative downward.

ColumnNeedMeaning
mdrequiredMeasured depth, ascending, e.g. every 3 m
tvdssrequiredTrajectory depth at that MD
toprequiredTop of target at that MD (shallower, e.g. -1262)
baserequiredBase of target (deeper than top, e.g. -1268)
gr, phi, rhob, rt, ropoptionalGR (API), porosity (v/v), density (g/cc), resistivity (ohm·m), ROP. Blanks are fine.

Zone rule per row: above target if tvdss > top, below if tvdss < base, otherwise in target. Rows are sorted by MD, non-numeric rows are skipped, at least 4 rows are needed.

Quick test: use “Load built-in sample”, or open this page with #csv-sample on the end of the link.
